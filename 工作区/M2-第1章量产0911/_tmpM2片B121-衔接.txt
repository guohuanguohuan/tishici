[158] 方法2 过⟦D⟧作⟦DE⊥AB⟧于⟦E⟧，⟦DF⊥AC⟧于⟦F⟧；
[159] 【图】
[160] ⟦∵∠BAD=∠CAD⟧，⟦∴DE=DF⟧；⟦∵(BA)/(AC)=(S_(△BAD))/(S_(△DAC))⟧，⟦(BD)/(DC)=(S_(△BAD))/(S_(△ABC))⟧； ⟦∴(BA)/(AC)=(BD)/(DC)⟧
